# Bug Reports – Quincy Lamar Website

## Overview
This document contains defects identified during manual testing of the Quincy Lamar website.

---

## Bug 1

**Bug ID:** BUG-001  
**Title:** CTA is not prominently placed above the fold  

**Pre-Condition:** User is on homepage  

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open homepage  
2. Observe visible content without scrolling  

**Expected Result:**  
A clear call-to-action is visible immediately upon page load  

**Actual Result:**  
Call-to-action elements are present but located mid-page, reducing visibility  

**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open  

---
